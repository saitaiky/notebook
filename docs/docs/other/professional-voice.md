## Finding Your Professional Voice
- **Understanding Professional Voice**: Focus on optimizing your voice for professional scenarios, recognizing that each voice is unique and can be improved.
- **Actor Training Techniques**:
    - **Meaning and Intention**: Apply meaning and intention to enhance voice effectiveness.
    - **Personalization**: Explore different techniques to find what suits your voice best.
- **Self-Compassion in Practice**: Approach voice improvement with curiosity, focusing on exploration rather than perfection.
- **Exploring Voice Options**: Aim to elevate your voice by experimenting with various techniques.

### Key Elements of Voice and Speech
- **Physicality in Voice**: Understand how tension, alignment, breath control, and resonance impact voice quality.
- **Audience Connection**:
    - **Intention and Body Language**: Define communication goals and use body language to influence audience perception.
    - **Eye Contact and Grounding**: Use eye contact and grounding techniques to enhance connection and confidence.
- **Impact of Language**:
    - **Articulation, Pitch, Pace, and Pausing**: Focus on these elements to make your speech clear and engaging.
- **Integration**: Combine physicality, connection, and language impact to master professional communication.

### Managing Tension in Speaking Situations
- **Fight or Flight Response**: Recognize how this natural response affects your voice during stressful situations.
- **Identifying Tension**:
    - **Knees, Abdomen, Jaw**: Understand how tension in these areas impacts your voice and presence.
- **Experimenting with Tension**: Practice identifying and reducing tension to improve vocal delivery.
- **Tension Release Techniques**: Future focus on methods to release tension for more effective communication.

### Building Confidence
- **Confidence as a Skill**: Confidence is both a feeling and a skill that can be cultivated with practice.
- **Physical and Psychological Aspects**:
    - **Body Positioning and Positive Self-Talk**: Use these to reinforce confidence.
- **Boosting Confidence**:
    - **Acknowledge Emotions**: Recognize and reframe nervousness.
    - **Prepare for Success**: Center yourself before important events.
    - **Grounding During Moments**: Focus on breath to manage nerves during presentations.

### Finding Confidence from Within
- **Challenge Limiting Beliefs**: Identify and reframe negative beliefs about your abilities.
- **Recognizing Unique Value**:
    - **Expertise and Personal Traits**: Reflect on what differentiates you and adds value.
- **Importance of Presentation**:
    - **Personal and Professional Growth**: Recognize the significance of your presentations.
- **Connecting with the Audience**: Find common ground to reduce intimidation.
- **Defining Success and Failure**: Set clear expectations and reframe failure as a learning opportunity.
