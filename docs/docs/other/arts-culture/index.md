Culture often becomes less precise when every term is forced into English. This collection therefore organises related guides by subject while preserving the Japanese and Chinese language used by their sources.

English introductions provide orientation. Original names, concepts, and passages remain where they carry historical or cultural meaning that a short translation cannot reproduce.

## Japanese gardens

Start with the [Japanese Gardens overview](/other/japanese-garden/) for the historical and aesthetic context. The detailed guides then cover:

- [日本園林的基本樣式](/other/japanese-garden/theory/types/) — the principal garden forms.
- [石組的含義和設計及建造方法](/other/japanese-garden/theory/stone/) — the symbolism and construction of stone arrangements.
- [池泉及瀑布的基礎](/other/japanese-garden/theory/water_waterfall/) — ponds, water, and waterfalls.
- [飛石](/other/japanese-garden/theory/stepping_stone/) — stepping stones as practical and aesthetic devices.
- [石灯籠](/other/japanese-garden/theory/lantern/) and [手水鉢と蹲踞](/other/japanese-garden/theory/chozubachi_tsukubai/) — lanterns, water basins, and tea-garden composition.
- [植栽技術](/other/japanese-garden/theory/tree/) and [竹](/other/japanese-garden/theory/bamboo/) — planting and bamboo craft.
- [日本庭園の変遷](/other/japanese-garden/theory/transformation/) — the historical development of Japanese gardens.
- [庭園見学](/other/japanese-garden/tour/) — a bilingual index of gardens to visit.

## Visual arts

- [Drawing](/other/drawing/) collects practical observations about proportion, value, light, and visual training.
- [Ukiyoe 浮世絵](/other/drawing/ukiyoe/) introduces major artists and works while retaining their original names.
- [Ukiyoe map 浮世絵地図](/other/drawing/ukitoe-map/) connects landscape prints to the places they depict.
- [Portrait](/other/drawing/portrait/) records focused studies of facial form and shadow.

## Food and drink

- [Kaiseki 懐石](/other/food-culture/kaiseki/) explains the courses, aesthetics, and terminology of Japanese multi-course dining.
- [Wine](/other/food-culture/wine/) introduces grape varieties, regions, and styles.

## History and place

- [History and Culture of the UK](/other/life-in-the-uk/) connects historical periods, institutions, customs, and symbols encountered in everyday British life.

## Language convention

These are not parallel translations. Each page keeps the language best suited to its source material. English navigation makes the collection discoverable; the original language remains canonical when translating it would lose precision.
