Interview preparation works best when it is connected to real technical understanding. Use this section to organise
evidence and communication after studying the relevant programming, architecture, Linux, or AWS topic.

## Preparation paths

- **Behavioural evidence:** build a
  [story bank](/software-development/interview/behavioral-question/building-story-bank/), then shape each example with
  [STAR](/software-development/interview/behavioral-question/star/).
- **Customer conversations:** practise [discovery](/software-development/interview/customer-interview/discovery/),
  [demonstrations](/software-development/interview/customer-interview/demo/), and
  [handling objections](/software-development/interview/customer-interview/handle-objection/).
- **Technical depth:** use the [technical-question index](/software-development/interview/technical-question/),
  [scaling questions](/software-development/interview/technical-question/scaling/), and the
  [system-design framework](/software-development/interview/technical-question/system-design/the-framework/).
- **Solution architecture:** connect the [solution architect notes](/software-development/interview/sa/) to the
  [system-design hub](/software-development/system-design/) and [AWS section](/aws/).

The aim is to explain a decision, its constraints, and its trade-offs—not simply repeat a service definition. A strong
answer can move from a general mechanism to Linux runtime behaviour and then to a concrete AWS implementation.
